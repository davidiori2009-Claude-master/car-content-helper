01 What this tool is

Car Content Helper (“the Tool”) is a private, single-operator content preparation utility. It helps its operator organise photographs and written details of vehicles offered for sale, and prepare them for publishing on the operator’s own social media accounts.

The Tool is not a public service. It is not offered to, sold to, or made available to any other person or organisation.

02 Who may use it

Only the operator who installed and configured the Tool may use it. The Tool is locked to a single authorised account and refuses all other requests.

03 What the Tool does

  • Receives vehicle photographs and text supplied by the operator
  • Organises the photographs into a consistent order
  • Removes third-party branding, such as dealership plate frames, from the photographs
  • Drafts a caption describing the vehicle
  • With the operator’s TikTok authorisation, uploads the prepared photographs to the operator’s own TikTok drafts

The Tool never publishes content. Every post is reviewed, edited and published manually by the operator inside the TikTok application.
