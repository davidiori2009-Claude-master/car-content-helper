Car Content Helper

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 August 2026

Car Content Helper is a private tool used by a single operator. It does not collect personal data from the public, has no users other than its operator, and sells nothing to anyone.

01 What data the Tool handles

The Tool handles only material the operator supplies to it directly:

  • Photographs of vehicles offered for sale
  • Text describing those vehicles, such as model, year, mileage and price
  • The operator’s own TikTok access token, used solely to upload prepared photographs to the operator’s own drafts

The Tool does not read, collect or store data belonging to any other TikTok user. It does not access followers, comments, messages, viewing history or analytics.

02 Where the data is stored

All photographs and text are stored locally on the operator’s own computer. Nothing is stored on a public server, and there is no shared database.

03 Third-party processing

To organise photographs and draft captions, the Tool sends the vehicle photographs and vehicle description text to the Anthropic API for processing. Anthropic’s handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Removal of third-party branding from photographs is performed locally on the operator’s computer, and those images are not sent anywhere for that step.

No data is sold, rented or shared with advertisers or any other third party.

04 TikTok data

The Tool requests the minimum TikTok permission required to upload content to the operator’s own drafts. The access token is stored only on the operator’s computer, in a local configuration file, and is never transmitted to anyone else.

The operator may revoke the Tool’s access at any time from TikTok’s account settings, which immediately ends all access.

05 Retention

Photographs and text are retained locally for as long as the operator finds them useful, and may be deleted by the operator at any time by removing the local files.

06 Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is not accessible to anyone other than its operator.

07 Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above reflects the current version.
