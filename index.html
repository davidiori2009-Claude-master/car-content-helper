Car Content Helper

A single-operator utility for preparing vehicle listing posts

Car Content Helper takes the photographs and written details of a vehicle that is offered for sale, prepares them into a consistent, publish-ready post, and places that post in its operator’s own TikTok drafts.

01 What it does

  1. The operator forwards a vehicle’s photographs and description to the tool.
  2. The tool reads the description and drafts a short caption: model, year, mileage, power and finance options.
  3. It sorts the photographs into a consistent order — exterior first, then interior, then detail shots.
  4. It removes third-party branding from the images, such as a dealership’s number plate surround.
  5. It uploads the finished set to the operator’s own TikTok drafts.
  6. The operator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, adds sound, and decides whether to publish.

02 How it uses TikTok

The tool requests one permission: the ability to upload content to the drafts of the account that authorised it. It uploads photographs and a caption, and nothing else.

The tool never publishes. Every post is reviewed and published by hand, by the operator, inside the TikTok app. Nothing reaches an audience without a person deciding to send it.

03 What it does not do

  • It has no users other than its operator, and is locked to a single authorised account.
  • It does not read followers, comments, messages, viewing history or analytics.
  • It does not post publicly, schedule posts, or act without the operator present.
  • It is not sold, licensed or offered to anyone else.

04 Where it runs

The tool runs locally on the operator’s own desktop computer. Photographs and text are stored on that machine. Branding removal is performed locally. Caption drafting and photo sorting use the Anthropic API; no other service receives the operator’s content.
